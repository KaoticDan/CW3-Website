who we are

CW3 was born from the diverse experiences and insights of its founders, each bringing a unique perspective to the challenges of building successful teams  in the Web3 space.

about CW3

Danyal Atif, the primary founder, drew from his extensive and varied background across multiple industries and roles. Through his journey, spanning C-suite positions, digital marketing, operations, and project management, Danyal recognized a crucial truth: regardless of the industry or the role, people are always the common denominator of success.

This insight was complemented by the expertise of co-founders Rime Salmi, with years of active involvement and a vast network in the blockchain space, and Samuel Lui, who brings innovative approaches to candidate management systems.

The founding team recognized a critical gap in the Web3 ecosystem. While the industry was brimming with innovative ideas, it often lacked the human capital  to bring these concepts to fruition. CW3 was established to bridge this gap, leveraging the founders' collective knowledge to not just fill positions, but to build teams capable of turning visionary ideas into reality.

In an industry where technology evolves at breakneck speed, CW3 was founded on  the principle that the true differentiator is the people behind the code.  Our mission became clear: to ensure that blockchain projects don't just hire employees, but build cohesive, long-lasting teams capable of navigating the exciting and unpredictable world of Web3.
